This mobile bank is derived from VSCO 2 CE Upright Piano samples.
Changes: select dyn2/rr1 and 23 alternate sample indices; use the official
MappingChart MIDI roots; trim leading silence with 2 ms pre-roll;
anti-aliased resampling to 24 kHz stereo; common gain adjustment;
10/7/4 second tail limits and 120 ms fade; 16-bit PCM WAV conversion.
No sustain loops or extra release/noise layers are included.
Exact source revision and source/output SHA-256 hashes: instrument.json.
