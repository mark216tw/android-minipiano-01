This mobile sound bank is derived from the above instrument.
Changes: select v10 velocity layer, trim using upstream onset offsets,
anti-aliased 48 kHz -> 24 kHz stereo resampling, common gain adjustment,
shorten tails with a 120 ms fade, and convert to 16-bit PCM WAV.
No original SFZ release/resonance/noise layers or sustain loops are included.
The exact source revision and source/output SHA-256 hashes are in instrument.json.
